If you’re accepted, you get everything from Fortune 100 Masterclasses to Tier 1 Fundraising to Live Cohort Coaching Calls to 24/7 ongoing async support. If accelerators take you from 0 to 1, The CEO Cohort takes you from 1 to n.

Every founder works directly with General Partner Jonathan Herzog. Jonathan is a successful ed-tech startup founder, has advised thousands of startups, including coach to OpenAI, Reddit, Coinbase, Flexport, and works closely with a small group of startups he personally hand-selects every batch.
