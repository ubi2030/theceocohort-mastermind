The Program  ·  Academic Experience

The 12-month MBA for founders.

The Herzog Business School MBA is the Herzog Graduate School of Business certification for tech founders and CEOs — from bootstrapped to public company, seed stage to Series D, across every industry and sector. It is built for leaders who want to close Fortune 100 deals and never have to raise a round again.

Over two semesters, founders move from heads-down product mode to scaled-up distribution mode. Semester 1 builds the required foundation every CEO needs. Semester 2 turns that foundation into live results through electives, field immersion and a capstone delivered inside your own company.
